I am a Postdoctoral researcher in the [Matsen Lab](https://matsen.fhcrc.org/) at Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center in Seattle. I work on building antibody language models.

Previously, I completed my PhD in Genomics and Computational Biology at the University of Pennsylvania, where I studied with [Prof. Junhyong Kim](https://kim.bio.upenn.edu/). My graduate work focused on understanding gene regulation using single cell multi-omics data, and decoding kidney development with multi-omics assays. I developed a tool to quantify snATAC-seq data uniformly for different technologies and estimate insertion rate (see [PIC publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-023-02103-7)). In addition, I developed a tool to conduct differential accessible region test in snATAC-seq data (see [PACS publication](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.07.30.551108v1)). Collaborating with Prof. Katalin Susztak, I also characterized the first kidney single cell open chromatin atlas for the developing and adult mouse (see [dev_kidney_atlas publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22266-1)).
