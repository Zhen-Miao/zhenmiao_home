Research Interests

  • Spatial transcriptomics and single-cell multiomics
    • Modeling coordinated cell-state changes across cell types and tissue sections.
    • Integrating single-cell multiomics to study cellular regulation and tissue organization.
  • Single-cell epigenomics
    • Separating latent chromatin accessibility from assay measurement and capture processes.
    • Testing the effects of multiple biological factors on chromatin accessibility.
  • Biological sequences
    • Combining language models with mechanistic models of B-cell receptor evolution.
    • Distinguishing mutation, codon preference, and amino-acid selection.
